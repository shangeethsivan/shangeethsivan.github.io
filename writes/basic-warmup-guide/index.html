Basic running warm-up

Five moves to wake up before you run, five to cool down after. Also: Java JIT and Android.

Before you run (dynamic)

1

Jumping jacks

30 seconds. Arms and legs open and close together.

2

Arm circles

10 forward, 10 back. Start small, then bigger.

3

Leg swings

10 each leg, front-to-back, hold a wall if needed.

4

High knees

20 total. Soft landings, tall posture.

5

Walking lunges

8 each side. Knee tracks over the front foot.

After you run (hold still)

1

Standing quad stretch

20–30s each side. Heel toward glute, knees together.

2

Standing hamstring stretch

20–30s each side. Hinge at the hip, long spine.

3

Calf stretch

20–30s each side. Hands on a wall, back heel down.

4

Hip-flexor lunge stretch

20–30s each side. Back knee down, tuck the pelvis.

5

Figure-four stretch

20–30s each side. Ankle on opposite knee, sit back.

General mobility routine only — not medical advice. Skip anything that hurts, and check with a clinician if you are unsure.
